  \caption{Neural Data Router (NDR; ICLR 2022), following Section~2.1 and
  Figure~5. A post-attention state $a_t$ feeds an MLP and sigmoid, producing a
  vector gate $g_t$. The candidate and previous state are mixed channel by
  channel. Every token has its own complete carry path. Geometric attention
  and the candidate FFN are abstracted within the shared NDR layer; its
  original encoder architecture is retained in this schematic.}
  \label{fig:residual-comparison-ndr}

\begin{figure*}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/loop-residual-comparison-v1/invariant-loop.pdf}
  \caption{Invariant Loop, following the implementation. Soft feedback $f_t$
  forms the candidate $c_t$. An MLP reads five statistics of the candidate,
  previous state and current/previous predictions. Its processed score is
  normalized against retained historical mass $\beta D_{t-1}$ to obtain the
  scalar $\gamma_t$. This mass enters normalization, not the MLP. Dashed
  control reaches the highlighted aggregate; solid carry paths preserve each
  token's own state. After reinjection and LayerNorm,
  $\mathrm{LN}_1(h_t+e)$ supplies the common next-loop Q/K/V input.
  Exact score processing and auxiliary recurrent state are specified in
  the accompanying notes. The soft-feedback component follows DiscoLoop.}
  \label{fig:residual-comparison-invariant}
\end{figure*}
