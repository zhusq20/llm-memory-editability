Layerwise representation, intervention, and writing measurements. Separate figures show Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3.5-2B, with all original layer indices retained. (a,d) Entity, question, and interaction variance shares of MLP down-projection inputs at the entity token and final prompt token, respectively; all 97/98 countries × eight questions. These shares describe dependence on inputs, not semantic decoding or causal localization. (b) Subject-end residual replacement after each decoder block on natural city-to-country questions, scored by teacher-forced exact greedy matching to the donor's answer. (e) Fraction of originally correct teacher-forced matches lost when the final prompt position's access to subject tokens is blocked in a moving window (five layers for Qwen3, four for Qwen3.5). Qwen3.5 uses attention masking and/or a mean-input replacement intervention in DeltaNet; these operations differ. (c) On the same prompts as a,d, a counterfactual-answer loss yields per-position norm-product fractions ||delta_p|| ||k_p|| / sum_q ||delta_q|| ||k_q||. Entity, final prompt and reply positions are shown; other positions are omitted. This is not the norm fraction of a summed parameter update. (f) On two separate novel-fact sets (82/102 facts per model per set), the descending gradient direction at the position predicting the first target-entity token is compared with the target's unembedding row and its centered neutral-context entity representation. Means are taken within fact across templates, then across facts. Solid/dashed lines denote the two fact sets, not confidence intervals. This position can occur inside the reply. Panels a,c,d share prompts; b,e and f provide complementary measurements on different tasks, not one matched causal trajectory. The final-layer absence of non-answer-position MLP gradients follows from the architecture.
