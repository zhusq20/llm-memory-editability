Qwen3-1.7B layer interventions on natural country questions. (a) Replace the subject-end residual state with the corresponding state from a different subject; plot the rate of answers matching the donor for two question templates. (b) Block attention from the final prediction position to the subject over a moving window of five layers; plot the fraction of originally correct answers made incorrect. Layers are zero-based. Panels use distinct evaluation sets and measure different quantities; they are not paired examples. Each curve is a saved single-model measurement, with no confidence interval computed here. Window overlap precludes assigning the effect to a single layer. These interventions support a finite range of layers in which changing the subject state can affect the answer, and a later attention-reading window; they do not locate all stored knowledge or prove a universal model mechanism.
